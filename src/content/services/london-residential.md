# London Residential Architecture

**Design your home. Live in it better.**

We design private homes across London — refurbishments, extensions, and new-build houses — with every decision rooted in light, proportion, and materiality.

We guide you through the full planning journey: pre-application discussions, planning submissions, and every stage in between. If you're not yet ready to design, our early-stage feasibility service helps you understand what's possible before committing.

---

## What's Covered

- Full **RIBA Stages 0–7** — from initial feasibility through to construction sign-off
- Pre-application discussions and planning submissions with local authorities
- Early-stage feasibility and appraisal to assess development potential and planning constraints

---

## Our Approach

Every project begins with a close study of the site — how light moves through it, how spaces connect, and what the building could become. We work closely with local authorities from an early stage, giving your project the best possible foundation for planning approval and construction.

Whether you're extending a Victorian terrace, refurbishing a period flat, or building a new home from the ground up, our process is structured, informed, and designed around you.

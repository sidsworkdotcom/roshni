# London Residential architecture

We design private homes across London, including refurbishments, extensions, and new-build houses, with an approach rooted in light, proportion, and materiality.

We work closely with local authorities on pre-application discussions and planning submissions, guiding projects through the planning process with a clear and informed strategy. An early-stage feasibility and appraisal service is available to assess development potential and planning constraints before design work begins.

Our services cover RIBA Stages 0–7, from feasibility and planning through to Building Control and construction.

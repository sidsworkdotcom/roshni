# Concept & International Work

**Strong architectural ideas, wherever your project is.**

We work with international clients on residential, wellness, and boutique hospitality projects — providing architectural services shaped around the stage and ambition of each project.

Whether you need clarity at the concept stage or continuity through to delivery, we adapt our role to suit your project, location, and goals.

---

## What We Can Provide

- Early-stage concept development and feasibility studies
- Architectural vision, design direction, and idea testing
- Site, climate, and context analysis
- Coordination with local consultants as projects progress
- Ongoing design guidance to maintain quality and intent through to delivery

---

## How We Work

Projects may remain as conceptual studies or progress into built work — the scope is determined by your objectives, location, and programme.

At early stages, we help establish a clear architectural vision, test ideas, and explore development approaches informed by context, climate, and lifestyle. As projects develop, our role can extend into full architectural design, consultant coordination, and design quality oversight.

In every case, our focus is on considered direction that supports informed decisions and creates a strong foundation for successful development.

## Ideal For

- International clients developing residential projects outside the UK
- Wellness retreats and boutique hospitality ventures at early concept stage
- Clients who need a trusted architectural voice before committing to full design

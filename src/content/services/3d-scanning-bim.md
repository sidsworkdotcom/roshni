# Measured Surveys & 3D Scanning

**Accurate information. Better decisions.**

We provide measured surveys using 3D laser scanning technology for architects, homeowners, and developers across London. This service can be commissioned independently and delivers accurate, reliable drawings to support design, planning, and construction.

Our surveys are particularly suited to existing buildings, including period properties and complex urban sites, where precise information is essential.

---

## Survey Outputs

Depending on project requirements, outputs may include:

- Floor plans, elevations, and sections
- Topographical information
- As-built drawings

The information produced forms a dependable base for feasibility studies, planning submissions, renovations, extensions, and construction coordination.

---

## Enquiries

To commission a measured survey, get in touch with:

1. Your property address
2. A brief description of the project
3. Any relevant timescales

We'll advise on the appropriate survey scope and provide a quotation.

# Measured Surveys & 3D Scanning

**Accurate information. Better decisions.**

Good design starts with reliable data. We provide measured surveys using 3D laser scanning technology for architects, homeowners, and developers across London — delivering precise, dependable drawings that support every stage of a project.

This service can be commissioned independently, without engaging us for full architectural services.

---

## What We Deliver

Survey outputs are tailored to your project requirements and may include:

- Floor plans, elevations, and sections
- Topographical information
- As-built drawings

These outputs form a reliable base for feasibility studies, planning submissions, renovations, extensions, and construction coordination.

---

## Particularly Suited To

- Existing buildings, including period and listed properties
- Complex urban sites where precision is critical
- Projects where existing drawings are unavailable, incomplete, or unreliable

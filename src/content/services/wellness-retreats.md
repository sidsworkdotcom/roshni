# Wellness & Retreat Architecture

We work on wellness-led projects including retreats, resorts, and residential developments where wellbeing is central to the brief. Our approach applies residential-scale thinking to larger environments, creating calm, considered places that support rest, retreat, and long-term use.

Our services may include:

- Site analysis and concept studies
- Early-stage masterplanning and spatial strategy
- Architectural design for retreat and wellness buildings
- Integration of landscape, environment, and setting

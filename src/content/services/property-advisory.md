# UK Property Advisory & Architectural Guidance for International Clients

**Your trusted guide to buying and renovating in London.**

We offer a dedicated advisory service for international clients seeking to purchase and renovate residential property in London. The service provides clear, independent guidance through the UK property process, from early-stage search and appraisal through to renovation and long-term occupation.

Acting as your architectural advisor, we help you understand the potential of prospective properties before purchase, advising on layout, renovation scope, planning considerations, and long-term suitability. We also connect you with a trusted network of London-based estate agents, solicitors, and specialist advisors experienced in working with international buyers.

Where a renovation or extension is proposed, our involvement can transition seamlessly into full architectural services, providing continuity from initial advice through to design, planning, and construction.

---

## How the Process Works

| Stage                                | What Happens                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Initial Consultation**          | A structured consultation on your objectives, budget, preferred locations, and long-term plans, with an overview of the London market and purchase process. |
| **2. Property Search Alignment**     | Introductions to suitable estate agents and advisors, so the search reflects your architectural, lifestyle, and investment requirements.                     |
| **3. Architectural Appraisal**       | High-level reviews of shortlisted properties, covering renovation potential, planning constraints, and strategic advice before you make an offer.            |
| **4. Legal & Purchase Coordination** | Introductions to solicitors experienced in London residential transactions for international buyers, with ongoing coordination during the purchase.         |
| **5. Design & Renovation**           | Where appropriate, progression into full architectural services for renovation, extension, or redevelopment.                                                 |

# UK Property Advisory & Architectural Guidance for International Clients

We offer a dedicated advisory service for international clients seeking to purchase and renovate residential property in London. The service provides clear, independent guidance through the UK property process, from early-stage search and appraisal through to renovation and long-term occupation.

Acting as an architectural advisor, we help clients understand the potential of prospective properties before purchase, advising on layout, renovation scope, planning considerations, and long-term suitability. We also connect clients with a trusted network of London-based estate agents, solicitors, and specialist advisors experienced in working with international buyers.

Where a renovation or extension is proposed, our involvement can transition seamlessly into full architectural services, providing continuity from initial advice through to design, planning, and construction.

This service is designed for clients who are not based in the UK and require a knowledgeable, trusted point of contact to guide them through the complexities of purchasing and renovating property in London.

## How the Process Works

1. Initial Consultation\
   A structured consultation to understand your objectives, budget, preferred locations, and long-term plans, alongside an overview of the London property market and purchase process.

2. Property Search Alignment\
   Introductions to suitable estate agents and advisors, ensuring the property search aligns with your architectural, lifestyle, and investment requirements.

3. Architectural Appraisal\
   High-level architectural reviews of shortlisted properties, including renovation potential, planning constraints, and strategic advice prior to making an offer.

4. Legal & Purchase Coordination\
   Introductions to solicitors experienced in London residential transactions for international buyers, with ongoing coordination during the purchase phase.

5. Design & Renovation\
   Where appropriate, progression into full architectural services for renovation, extension, or redevelopment.

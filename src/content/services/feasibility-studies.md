# Pre-Purchase Feasibility

### Buy with clarity. Design with confidence.

A pre-purchase feasibility study helps you understand what’s possible *before* you commit to a property. We review the site and your goals, identify opportunities and constraints, and outline a clear direction—so you can make a confident, informed decision.

Ideal for: luxury residences, wellness retreats, boutique resort projects, and high-value renovations.

## What you’ll get

- Feasibility overview of what can realistically be achieved on the site
- Opportunity + risk assessment (planning, site constraints, access, services, budget sensitivity)
- High-level space planning options (how the layout could work)
- Massing / concept direction (scale, footprint, key moves)
- Indicative budget + timeline guidance *(high-level, not a final quote)*
- Next-step roadmap if you proceed (design phases, approvals, consultant needs)

## How it works

- Discovery Call (your brief, lifestyle / brand goals, timeline)
- Site + Documentation Review (agent pack, drawings, photos, planning context)
- Feasibility Study (options + key recommendations)
- Presentation + Q&A (walkthrough and clear next steps)

## What we need from you

- Property address/location and any listing details
- Any available plans, survey, or agent pack
- Your wishlist (rooms, style, wellness requirements, resort amenities, etc.)
- Your target budget range and ideal timeline

## Outcomes

By the end of this service, you’ll know:

- whether the property supports your vision,
- what compromises (if any) are likely,
- and the smartest path forward.

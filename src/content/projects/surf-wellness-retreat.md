This project is an Eco-Resort, designed as the ultimate surfer's retreat. The design philosophy merges secluded luxury with a profound commitment to sustainable architecture, seamlessly integrating into the natural beachfront landscape. The project is an architectural study in curvilinear forms, utilizing local, raw materials to create a private, harmonized environment.

### BIOPHILIC HARMONY

The core concept centers on "Tropical Eco-Luxury," where architecture is not an imposition on the land, but an organic extension of it. The master plan employs winding pebble pathways that weave through clustered, circular accommodation units, fostering a sense of discovery and community while respecting personal privacy. All structures feature expansive open-air designs to embrace the coastal environment.

### SUSTAINABLE MATERIA PALETTE

The visual language of the resort is defined by its material palette, which prioritizes locality and a low carbon footprint.

- Earth Construction: A key feature is the 30cm thick mud and rammed-earth wall system, seen in our detailed material studies. This method provides natural insulation and a deeply textural, earthy aesthetic.
- Locally Sourced Bamboo: This resilient, renewable material is used extensively for the dramatic, organic roof structures, such as the Main Pavilion, and for detailed wall elements.
- Raw Stone and Thatch: These natural elements are integrated for flooring and roofing, enhancing the biophilic integration.

### PROJECT BY THE NUMBERS

- Estimated Total Site Area: ~3,500 m² (for lush development and privacy)
- Total Accommodation Units: 7 (for a highly intimate and exclusive feel)
- Accommodations Layout: Mix of clustered units (e.g., Villa 1, 2, 3), some with private pools.

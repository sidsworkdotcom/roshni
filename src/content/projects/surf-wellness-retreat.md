An eco-resort designed as the ultimate surfer's retreat. The design merges secluded luxury with a deep commitment to sustainable architecture, integrating seamlessly into the natural beachfront landscape. It is a study in curvilinear forms, using local, raw materials to create a private, harmonious environment.

## Biophilic Harmony

The core concept is "Tropical Eco-Luxury", where architecture is an organic extension of the land rather than an imposition on it. Winding pebble pathways weave through clustered, circular accommodation units, fostering discovery and community while respecting privacy. All structures are designed as expansive open-air spaces that embrace the coastal environment.

## Sustainable Material Palette

The resort's visual language is defined by materials chosen for locality and a low carbon footprint.

- **Earth Construction:** 30 cm thick mud and rammed-earth walls provide natural insulation and a deeply textural, earthy aesthetic.
- **Locally Sourced Bamboo:** this resilient, renewable material forms the dramatic organic roof structures, including the Main Pavilion, as well as detailed wall elements.
- **Raw Stone & Thatch:** used for flooring and roofing to deepen the biophilic integration.

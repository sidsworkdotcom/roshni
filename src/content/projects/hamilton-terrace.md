This project is a ground-floor flat renovation located on Hamilton Terrace in the St John’s Wood Conservation Area, undertaken in collaboration with Aisha Subhani Design. The scheme transforms the space into a three-bedroom, three-ensuite home with impressive ceiling heights of 3 m in the bedrooms and up to 4 m in the living areas, enhancing the sense of volume and light throughout.

The interior design is carefully curated with a rustic aesthetic, celebrating craftsmanship and creating unique, characterful spaces that reflect the client’s appreciation for artisanal detail. Every element of the flat has been considered to balance functionality with a warm, distinctive atmosphere, resulting in a home that is both refined and inviting

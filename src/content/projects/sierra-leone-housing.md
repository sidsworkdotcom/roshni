This housing scheme is designed as a contemporary tropical community that responds directly to the local climate, landscape, and modern lifestyle needs. The architecture embraces natural ventilation, shaded outdoor spaces, and passive cooling strategies to create comfortable homes suited to the region's warm conditions.

Clean modern forms combine with natural materials (timber, stone, textured render) and lush tropical planting to create a warm, timeless aesthetic. Rooftop terraces, courtyards, and landscaped gardens promote wellness, natural light, and a strong connection between indoor and outdoor living.

At both the architectural and urban scale, the project is envisioned as a sustainable, wellness-focused neighbourhood that balances privacy, community, and contemporary living within a distinctly West African context.

This thoughtfully renovated terrace house has been reimagined to balance light, space, and everyday wellbeing. A carefully designed basement scheme extends the living area below ground, introducing a calm, insulated retreat that enhances both thermal comfort and acoustic privacy—ideal for rest, focus, or quiet family time.

At ground level, the home opens into a bright, airy kitchen and dining space where large glazing and overhead rooflights draw in natural daylight throughout the day. The seamless connection to the outdoor terrace encourages indoor-outdoor living, supporting fresh air flow and a closer relationship with nature.

From a wellness perspective, the design prioritizes simplicity, natural materials, and soft, neutral tones to create a soothing atmosphere. Generous daylight, improved ventilation, and uncluttered spatial flow contribute to a sense of mental clarity and relaxation, making the home not just functional, but restorative for daily life.

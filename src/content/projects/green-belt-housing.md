A sensitive development of ten homes set within the Green Belt in South Buckinghamshire. Designed to respect the historic character of the site, the scheme takes a low-density approach, with homes thoughtfully spaced to preserve a sense of openness and connection to the surrounding landscape.

Generous green spaces, tree-lined routes, and shared landscaped areas form the heart of the design, creating a calm and inviting environment. The architecture draws on local vernacular traditions, so each home complements its historic context while offering modern, comfortable living.

Sustainability is central to the scheme. Homes incorporate water-reuse strategies such as rainwater harvesting and greywater systems, while renewable energy generation allows the development to give back to the grid.

The result is a considered collection of homes that harmonises with its setting, offering low-density, sustainable living in a tranquil, village-like environment.

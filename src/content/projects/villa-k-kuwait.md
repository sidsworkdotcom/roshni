## The Project

Situated on a prominent corner lot within an exclusive private compound, Villa K is a luxury multi-level residence that balances classical monumentality with an airy, modern lifestyle. Clad entirely in honed light travertine, the architecture replaces solid volumes with deep setbacks and a striking rhythm of double-height arched openings.

## The Design

The pale stone facade acts as a protective shield against the desert sun while framing towering windows that flood the formal reception spaces with natural light. The ground level is designed for hosting, with floor-to-ceiling sliding glass walls that open directly onto a stone-paved entertainment terrace and a sleek linear lap pool.

Upstairs, the private family quarters step back to reveal an expansive wrap-around master balcony. Bordered by delicate black metalwork, this upper terrace provides a secluded, naturally shaded outdoor lounge overlooking the oasis below.

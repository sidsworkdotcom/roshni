### The Project

Situated on a prominent corner lot within an exclusive private compound, Villa K is a luxury multi-level residence that masterfully balances classical monumentality with airy, modern lifestyle. Clad entirely in a honed light travertine, the architecture replaces traditional solid volumes with deep setbacks and a striking rhythm of double-height arched openings.

### The Design

The building’s pale stone facade acts as a protective shield against the harsh desert sun while framing towering windows that flood the formal internal reception spaces with natural light. The ground level is engineered for upscale hosting, featuring floor-to-ceiling sliding glass walls that open directly onto a stone-paved entertainment terrace and a sleek, linear lap pool.

Upstairs, the private family quarters step back to reveal an expansive, wrap-around master balcony. Bordered by delicate black metalwork, this upper terrace provides a secluded, naturally shaded outdoor lounge that overlooks the oasis below.
